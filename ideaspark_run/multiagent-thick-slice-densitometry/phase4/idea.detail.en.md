# Certifying Emphysema Densitometry from Thick-Slice CT by Residual Arbitration

**Method.** ARC-CT (Arbitrated Reconstruction with Certificates for CT)

## Motivation
**Problem framing.** Quantitative CT densitometry is a threshold statistic: LAA-950 counts the fraction of lung voxels below -950 HU, and Perc15 reads the 15th percentile of the same histogram. Thick-slice acquisition averages along the through-plane axis, which narrows that histogram and therefore biases both numbers — an effect measured repeatedly across slice thickness, kernel and dose (openalex:W4404646478 for the restoration side; the diagnostic-side literature documents the bias but stops there). Two research lines respond. The restoration line synthesises thin slices from thick ones and is now validated multi-centre on DIAGNOSTIC endpoints (openalex:W4404646478) with a real-paired benchmark that exposes a simulated-versus-real domain gap (dblp:journals/corr/abs-2206-06253). The densitometry line leaves the image alone and corrects the scalar: kernel conversion to a reference protocol (openalex:W4407122143), or statistical correction of the density summary for volume, noise and inter-scanner bias.

Neither line can answer the question a trial actually asks. Because the map from a reconstruction to a threshold statistic is many-to-one, a global attenuation displacement and a genuine recovery of the low-attenuation tail produce the same LAA-950 and are indistinguishable to a voxel-wise or perceptual objective, to PSNR/SSIM, and to a reader study. This is not hypothetical: in our preliminary run, fine-tuning a densitometry-guarded restoration model on real 1 mm/5 mm pairs improved LAA-950 mean absolute error from 0.56 to 0.41 percentage points and concordance from 0.47 to 0.84 — and a tensor-level comparison showed that the ONLY weight that moved beyond float32 noise was the output bias, by -12.2 HU. Replacing that single scalar in the un-fine-tuned model reproduced the entire gain, while the reconstruction's lung standard deviation stayed at 54.9 HU against the reference's 58.6 HU.

The structural reason is that the reconstruction's own physics is never held accountable. The acquisition operator is assumed — nominally 5 mm, when a fit on 50 real pairs gives an effective width of 6.25 mm — so the data-consistency residual conflates operator error with reconstruction error, and a displacement large enough to move the endpoint hides inside it. The model-based lines that do relax the operator assumption (arxiv:2503.21501v1, arxiv:2403.04847v2, semanticscholar:0d3795c98a2a83d53704e5965e08461bc452dfc1) recover physical parameters for their own sake and never ask what a per-scan estimate must be accountable to, while the safety-oriented line bounds edits in the image domain (arxiv:2605.16458v1), which neither implies nor detects a bound on the functional that is actually read.

**Why now.** Three things converged inside the last twelve months. First, thick-slice restoration crossed from image quality into clinical validation (openalex:W4404646478, npj Digital Medicine 2024), so its outputs are now candidates for measurement, not only for viewing. Second, the field acquired the instruments to name this failure: a cross-modality benchmark showing that deployed reconstruction networks collapse under operator mismatch (arxiv:2603.04538v1, 2026-03), and a physics-informed benchmark for detecting and grading CT degradation (arxiv:2605.16431v1, 2026-05) — both measure the disease, neither closes the loop back into the reconstruction or into a clinical quantity. Third, the agentic turn reached medical imaging (arxiv:2605.22208v2, arxiv:2606.29763v1, 2026), which makes it timely to ask what a multi-solver decomposition must own to be more than pipeline orchestration. Meanwhile the only public real-paired 1 mm/5 mm chest CT benchmark was released in full (dblp:journals/corr/abs-2206-06253), so the sim-versus-real question can now be answered on public data instead of behind institutional walls.

**Why prior work stopped.**
- `openalex:W4404646478` (npj Digital Medicine 2024): Built and multi-centre-validated a convolution-transformer network that synthesises 1 mm chest CT from 5 mm input, showing improved reader diagnosis and improved downstream AI performance.
  - _Did not do_: Never evaluated a densitometric endpoint — LAA-950, Perc15 and the lung attenuation distribution appear nowhere in the paper — so nothing distinguishes a reconstruction that restores the histogram from one that relocates it.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing measurement: their success criterion is diagnostic, and a diagnostic criterion is insensitive to a global attenuation offset that moves a threshold count.
- `dblp:journals/corr/abs-2206-06253` (International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer-Assisted Intervention 2022): Released the first real-paired 1 mm/5 mm chest CT benchmark and demonstrated that models trained on simulated degradation transfer poorly to real thick series.
  - _Did not do_: Left the gap as a PSNR/SSIM deficit without attributing it to a physical cause or attaching a clinical quantity to it.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing mechanism: the benchmark has no per-scan estimate of the acquisition, so the observed gap cannot be decomposed into operator error and reconstruction error.
- `arxiv:2603.04538v1` (arXiv 2026-03): Constructed a cross-modality benchmark quantifying how far reconstruction networks fall when the assumed forward operator deviates from the physical one.
  - _Did not do_: Did not feed the mismatch estimate back into reconstruction, and did not connect it to any downstream measurement whose bias the mismatch must explain.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing mechanism: the benchmark's unit of analysis is the network's image error, so a mismatch that matters only through a functional of the output has no place to be reported.
- `arxiv:2503.21501v1` (arXiv 2025): Inferred image and measurement operator jointly under a generative prior over operators, so a blind inverse problem can be solved without calibration.
  - _Did not do_: Did not construct the projection of the residual onto the direction that moves a downstream functional, so an operator error and an image offset remain exchangeable inside one posterior.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing measurement: with no functional to defend, there is no reason to privilege the mean direction of the residual over any other.
- `arxiv:2605.16458v1` (arXiv 2026-05): Argued that a restoration model in a safety-sensitive setting is not free to rewrite its input, and constrained the magnitude, location and stability of its edits.
  - _Did not do_: Bounded edits in the image domain only; a small, spatially diffuse, entirely admissible edit is exactly what moves a -950 HU threshold count.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing assumption-relaxation: the safety criterion is stated over voxels, not over the functional the clinician reads, so the bound cannot be transferred to the measurement.
- `openalex:W4407122143` (Tuberculosis & respiratory diseases 2025): Converted reconstruction kernels across hospitals to one reference setting and showed the resulting emphysema extent correlates better with clinical parameters.
  - _Did not do_: Harmonised the kernel only, left slice thickness untouched, and fitted one global mapping rather than estimating what the current scan's acquisition actually was.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing tool: without a per-scan estimate of the acquisition there is nothing to harmonise TOWARD except a reference protocol that archived data does not have.
- `arxiv:2605.17518v1` (arXiv 2026-05): Coupled Bayesian spectral deconvolution with an expert-reasoning loop that revises the physical model when automated deconvolution fails on unknown background or noise.
  - _Did not do_: Bound neither the reasoner's proposals to a data-consistency contract nor the loop to a quantity of record, so a disagreement between reasoner and measurement has no adjudicator.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing mechanism: the loop optimises the fit of a 1-D model, and there is no functional whose correctness the revised model must still defend.
- `arxiv:2605.22208v2` (arXiv 2026): Built a self-evolving multimodal-LLM restoration agent that selects tools and their application order for coupled degradations, learning from past trajectories.
  - _Did not do_: Spent agency on tool selection over an existing restoration toolbox; no agent owns a physical estimand, nothing binds the plan to the observation, and no measured quantity has to survive it.
  - _Structural reason_: Missing assumption-relaxation: with the degradation model outside the agents' remit, the plan can only be judged by the restored image, which is the very criterion that cannot see a displacement.

**What changes when the gap closes.** A densitometric value read off a restored thick-slice volume becomes reportable evidence rather than a number of unknown provenance: each scan carries a per-scan statement of how much of its LAA-950 is attributable to a global attenuation displacement and how much of the observation the identified acquisition cannot explain, so a multi-centre study can decide scan-by-scan whether a value may be pooled instead of relying on protocol paperwork. It also makes the restoration literature cumulative in a way it currently is not — an effective slice width and an unexplained-residual fraction are comparable across papers, scanners and thicknesses, whereas a PSNR on a private cohort is not. Finally it gives the field a way to test, rather than assert, that a restoration recovered structure: any method claiming tail recovery can be asked for the same certificate.

## Method
**Pipeline.** A thick-slice chest CT series arrives with no phantom, no reference protocol and no thin-slice counterpart. A non-learned operator solver first asks what acquisition could have produced it, fitting an effective through-plane width and noise level from the series itself and reporting how much of the observation that fitted physics cannot explain. The reconstruction network is then conditioned on that identification rather than on a nominal slice width, and returns a 1 mm volume. A moment-based densitometry solver takes the reconstruction back through the identified acquisition and splits the disagreement with the observation into a global displacement term — the one direction slab averaging leaves invariant, so it is readable no matter what width was fitted — and a structural remainder, adding a protocol-calibrated tail-recovery term when a calibration for this protocol exists. The arbiter reads the resulting certificate and returns certify, flag or abstain together with the emphysema index, so the number leaves the pipeline with its provenance attached.

### M-OP
*Work out, from the thick scan alone, how it was actually acquired, and say how much of it that explanation misses.*

1. **Identify the acquisition per scan (operator solver)** (`S2`)
   - Implement the fit as a loop over w in numpy.arange(2.00, 12.001, 0.25): build the normalised sampled Gaussian $k_{w}$ with radius ceil(3*sigma(w)), convolve along z with replicate padding, average-pool by r, and record the mean squared error against y over the eroded lung interior. Return $w_{hat} = argmin,$ the full error profile (so its curvature and any grid-endpoint saturation are inspectable), $sigma_{hat} = 1.4826 *$ MAD of the z first differences inside the lung set divided by sqrt(2), and rho $= ||A_{w_{hat}}(x_{hat}) -$ y||_2 / ||y - mean(y)||_2. Cost is 41 blur-and-pool passes, seconds per case on GPU. 【author decision: whether $x_{tilde}$ for the fit is the trilinear up-sampling of y or the model's own current output - the two give different bias, and the run must report which was used】

*The through-plane acquisition model: a normalised Gaussian slice-sensitivity profile of full-width-half-maximum w applied along z, followed by an average over r thin slices per thick slice.*
$$ A_w(x)[k] = \frac{1}{r}\sum_{j=0}^{r-1} (k_w * x)[rk + j], \qquad \sum_i k_w[i] = 1 \tag{1} $$


*The operator solver: a grid fit of the effective width from the observed thick series itself, plus the fraction of the observation the fitted physics cannot explain.*
$$ \hat{w} = \arg\min_{w \in \mathcal{W}} \|A_w(\tilde{x}) - y\|_2^2, \qquad \rho = \frac{\|A_{\hat{w}}(\hat{x}) - y\|_2}{\|y - \bar{y}\|_2} \tag{2} $$

   - _Why:_ Every certificate term is computed under this estimate; a nominal width both hides a displacement inside the residual and mis-conditions the network.
2. **Condition the reconstruction on the identified acquisition** (`S3`)
   - Replace the constant conditioning vector in the identification module: where the current code computes $\mathit{fwhm\_to\_sigma}(5.0)$ and broadcasts it, feed the per-sample $(w_{hat}, sigma_{hat})$ pair through the existing FiLM path, normalised to zero mean and unit variance using statistics computed once over the training set (store them with the checkpoint so inference matches). Everything downstream is unchanged, so the effect is visible directly in $x_{hat}$ and in every statistic computed from it.
   - _Why:_ Separates identification-for-conditioning from identification-for-auditing, which ablation (c) then measures.

### M-CERT
*Turn the leftover disagreement into a small set of named numbers, one of which is readable no matter what acquisition was estimated.*

3. **Fit the tail-recovery calibration on training pairs only** (`S4`)
   - Fit the tail-recovery relation as a ridge regression of $Var_{z}(x)$ on the features $[Var_{z}(y), w_{hat}, sigma_{hat}, Var_{z}(y)*w_{hat}]$ over training pairs only (both arms), with 5-fold grouping BY CASE so no case appears in both fit and evaluation folds; $sigma_{v}$ is the standard deviation of the held-out residuals. Refuse to ship the term when $sigma_{v}$ exceeds the between-case standard deviation of $Var_{z}(x)$ itself, i.e. when the relation carries no information. Persist (coefficients, $sigma_{v},$ protocol tag) next to the checkpoint. 【author decision: what defines a protocol tag - scanner model, kernel, nominal thickness, or the tuple - since it sets when a refit is required】

*The displacement term and the invariance it rests on: over the eroded interior $L_{e}$ of the lung set, the slab average preserves the mean for every width, so a global attenuation offset is identified independently of the width estimate.*
$$ \hat{\delta} = \operatorname*{mean}_{L_e}\big(A_{\hat{w}}(\hat{x}) - y\big), \qquad \operatorname*{mean}_{L_e} A_w(x) = \operatorname*{mean}_{L_e} x \;\; \forall w \tag{3} $$

   - _Why:_ The closed-form attenuation identity is false by an order of magnitude on real data, so the tail-recovery evidence must be empirical and scoped.
4. **Compute the certificate (densitometry solver)** (`S5`)
   - Build the thick-grid lung set L from the HU window $(\mathit{hu\_air\_floor}, \mathit{hu\_lung\_ceiling}) =$ (-990, -500), erode it with $scipy.ndimage.binary_{erosion}$ using an in-plane structuring element of radius $e_{mask} = 8$ and a z element of $ceil(3*sigma(w_{max})/r)$ slices, then compute $delta_{hat} = mean$ over $L_{e}$ of $(A_{w_{hat}}(x_{hat}) -$ y) in HU, $rho_{struct} =$ ||R $- delta_{hat}||_{2} /$ ||y - mean(y)||_2, and $s = (v_{hat} - Var_{z}(x_{hat}))/sigma_{v}$ when a calibration for this protocol exists (else s = UNAVAILABLE). Emit ABSTAIN when $|L_{e}| =$ 0. Write one CSV row per case with all six quantities plus LAA-950 and Perc15, so the endpoint and its evidence are never separable in the record.

*The single new training term: the displacement penalty, which bills the free lunch the trajectory guardrail otherwise takes.*
$$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{vox}} + w_{dc}\mathcal{L}_{dc} + w_{traj}\mathcal{L}_{traj} + w_{nps}\mathcal{L}_{nps} + \lambda_\delta |\hat{\delta}| \tag{4} $$

   - _Why:_ This is the typed artifact that crosses the boundary between the learned and the physical parts of the pipeline.
5. **Validate the estimators on a known-perfect reconstruction** (`S6`)
   - Run step S5 verbatim with $x_{hat}$ := the TRUE thin volume on every held-out real pair, and record the per-term distribution: $delta_{hat}$ should concentrate near its floor (measured -0.55 HU on one real pair, with the width-sweep spread 0.04 HU over w in [4,10] mm), s near zero in units of $sigma_{v}.$ Any term whose null distribution is wider than its own threshold is reported as an estimator bias in the paper and is NOT used to gate that arm. This is a pure evaluation pass: no training, no gradients.

*The arbiter: a feasibility verdict on the certificate rather than a score, naming the dominant violated term when it flags.*
$$ \text{verdict} = \begin{cases} \textsf{certified} & |\hat{\delta}| \le \tau_\delta \wedge \rho_{\text{struct}} \le \tau_\rho \wedge |s| \le \tau_s \\ \textsf{flagged} & \text{otherwise} \\ \textsf{abstain} & |L_e| = 0 \end{cases} \tag{5} $$

   - _Why:_ This check is what exposed the two defects in the first version of the mechanism, and it is cheap enough to be mandatory.

### M-GATE
*Penalise the cheap shift during training, and at deployment attach a certify / flag / abstain verdict to the reported emphysema value.*

6. **Add the displacement penalty and re-train** (`S7`)
   - Add $lambda_{delta} * |delta_{hat}|$ to the existing composite loss. $delta_{hat}$ must be computed with the same differentiable operator used in the data-consistency term so the gradient reaches the network; detach $w_{hat}$ (the fit is not differentiated through, which keeps the operator solver a sound solver rather than a second learned branch). Train with the existing recipe (gradient-norm clipping at 1.0, weight EMA 0.999) and sweep $lambda_{delta}$ over {0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10}.
   - _Why:_ It bills the one direction the trajectory guardrail could otherwise use to buy densitometric accuracy for free.
7. **Arbitrate and gate the reported value** (`S8`)
   - Implement the arbiter as a pure function certificate -> {certified, flagged(term), abstain}, with thresholds $tau_{delta} = 2$ HU, $tau_{rho} =$ 0.15, $tau_{s} = 2 sigma_{v},$ each swept over the stated range; report the operating characteristic (fraction certified versus achieved endpoint error) rather than a single threshold choice, since the useful output is the trade-off curve a trial can pick from.
   - _Why:_ Converts a disagreement between assumed physics and observation into a statement about the number a clinician reads.

### M-EVID
*Show the decomposition is what does the work, and that it survives a different slice thickness.*

8. **Build real thin/thick pairs and a matched simulated arm** (`S1`)
   - Read each paired series with SimpleITK, un-normalise to HU when the source ships normalised values (RPLHR-CT stores (HU+1024)/3072 clipped to [0,1]; verify per case by checking that the air peak lands at -1024 HU and the mediastinal soft-tissue peak at +30..+60 HU), clip to [-1000, 200] HU, compute the lung bounding box on the thin volume by 3D connected-component labelling of voxels < -400 HU with in-plane-border-touching components removed, apply that box to both series (z indices divided by r for the thick one), estimate the slab offset by minimising $||A_{w}(thin) - thick||^{2}$ over integer shifts in [-4, +4] thick slices, and trim so $D_{thin} = r * D_{thick}$ exactly. Write <case>_thin.npy / <case>_thick.npy plus one audit row per case (offset, match MSE, lung fraction). The simulated arm reuses the same thin crops with $A_{w}$ applied at a known w drawn per case from a stated range.
   - _Why:_ The confound-isolating separation the audit leg owes needs two arms that differ only in whether the acquisition is known.
9. **Evaluate five arms under one harness** (`S9`)
   - One evaluation script, five arms, identical data loader, identical lung masks, identical patch geometry: trilinear up-sampling; the existing restoration model; that model fine-tuned on real pairs; that model with ONLY its output bias shifted by the value fitted on a small number of real pairs; and ARC-CT. Report LAA-950 MAE and Lin's concordance, Perc15 MAE, PSNR, plus the full certificate per case. The harness must refuse to print an endpoint without its certificate columns. 【author decision: how many real pairs the bias-only arm may use for its single scalar - the arm's whole point is that it needs very few, so the number is a claim, not a detail】
   - _Why:_ The bias-only arm is the positive control that makes the hook's claim checkable.
10. **Ablate the decomposition** (`S10`)
   - Four ablation runs: (a) $w_{hat}$ frozen at 5.00 mm everywhere (operator solver removed) - expect the displacement term to be absorbed into rho and the arm to lose its separation power; (b) $lambda_{delta} = 0$; (c) conditioning removed while the certificate is still computed; (d) $e_{mask}$ swept over {4, 6, 8, 10, 12} at evaluation time only. Each ablation reports the same table as S9 so the marginal effect of each solver is read off directly.
   - _Why:_ Isolates the marginal contribution of each solver, which is what separates a decomposition from an assembly.
11. **Repeat at 8 mm without retuning** (`S11`)
   - Re-run the whole pipeline with r = 8 and the simulated 8 mm arm (the 8 mm real pairs are not public), keeping every threshold and hyper-parameter at its 5 mm value; the operator solver should return a wider $w_{hat}$ without any code change. Report the same joint table. 【author decision: whether to also claim 8 mm real-pair performance - the public data does not support it, so the honest scope is simulated 8 mm plus the 5 mm real result】
   - _Why:_ Cross-thickness transfer is the practical reason the operator is identified rather than assumed.
12. **Report certificate distributions per arm** (`S12`)
   - Plot the per-case distribution of (rho, $rho_{struct}, delta_{hat},$ s) for the simulated arm against the real arm, and regress the per-case endpoint error on $rho_{struct}$; the claim under test is that the unexplained residual is what predicts the simulated-to-real gap, so it needs the scatter plus its correlation, not a mean comparison.
   - _Why:_ Shows whether the residual the identified operator cannot explain is what separates simulated from real performance.
