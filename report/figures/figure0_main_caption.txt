Fig. 1 | Restoring emphysema densitometry from thick-slice chest CT.

a, Method. The 5 mm series y, the only series available at a site without
thin-slice reconstruction, passes through a base predictor to give x0, and the
scaled residual u = (x - x0)/s_r is sampled by flow matching with an explicit
Euler integration from t = 0 to t = 1, each step projected back onto the set
consistent with the measurement. The forward operator A_w, a through-plane
Gaussian of full width at half maximum w followed by averaging over r = 5
slices, both constrains that projection and supplies the three reference-free
quantities: the global attenuation displacement delta, the structural residual
rho_struct, and the through-plane variance term s. Because A_w preserves slab
means, these are computable without any 1 mm reference. Hatching marks the
modules that depend on the operator.

b, Coronal views of one examination from the public paired cohort, the plane in
which 5 mm and 1 mm acquisition differ. Columns are the 5 mm series upsampled,
CTHNet, SCTE-R, and the 1 mm reference; the lower row magnifies the boxed
parenchymal region. The 5 mm series shows through-plane staircase artefact,
CTHNet smooths the parenchymal texture, and SCTE-R reproduces a texture
comparable to the reference.

c, Fraction of lung voxels below a given attenuation, on a logarithmic vertical
axis, for the same examination. Dashed vertical lines mark the two standard
cut-offs. The 5 mm and CTHNet curves fall below the reference throughout the
low-attenuation region; SCTE-R follows the reference.

d, Peak signal-to-noise ratio against the absolute LAA-950 bias in the external
cohort (n = 444). The horizontal dashed line marks the mean bias of the three
non-generative arms: 3.2 dB separates the 5 mm series from CTHNet while the bias
falls by 0.5 percentage points. The curved arrow marks the distance covered by
SCTE-R from the unreconstructed series, 95% of the bias, at a lower PSNR.
Circles are generative arms, squares are the others.

e, Bias and 95% limits of agreement for LAA-950 (n = 444). Points are the bias,
horizontal bars the limits, and the dashed line zero bias.

f, Global attenuation displacement measured directly (n = 444). Bars are the
mean and error bars the standard deviation. The deterministic regression sits at
-16.32 +/- 0.65 HU; a standard deviation of 0.65 HU on a -16 HU effect
identifies the displacement as a property of the estimator rather than
case-level noise.

g, Fraction of examinations passing the competence indicator in four
model-by-domain combinations. Hatched fill marks the domain in which the
densitometric endpoints actually failed. The sharp-kernel data lie inside the
physical calibration set, so what separates the columns is whether the model has
seen this kind of data.

Panels b and c come from the public paired cohort, where reconstructions are
held locally; panels d to g report the external cohort. These are not the same
patients.
